HOW THE DEMO SCORES WORK

Comparable inputs, visible assumptions.

For each input, values are winsorized at the cross-sectional 5th and 95th percentiles, converted to population z-scores, and clipped to the range −3 to +3. Inputs that are naturally lower-is-better are direction-reversed before scoring. Each factor score is the mean of its available component z-scores; missing values are not replaced with zero. At least five usable peers are required for a component score.

In this demo, those operations are applied to generated numbers only. Beta and residual-volatility values here are synthetic inputs; this demo does not run the OLS regression. A production study would also need point-in-time financial reports, corporate-action-adjusted prices, delisted issuers, a consistent IDX universe, source provenance, and out-of-sample evaluation.
