What data should I upload?

For the full set of available factors and custom axes, provide issuer prices, IHSG prices, and company-report ratios. Select the CSV files together or put them in a ZIP. Files are read and calculated in this browser.

  • Issuer prices: prices.csv with ticker,date,close. Daily OHLCV files are accepted, but only closing prices are used. If both adjusted and ordinary closes are present, adjusted close is preferred. At least 253 closes per issuer are needed for 12–1 momentum and SMA200 distance.
  • Market benchmark: ihsg.csv with date,close (or an IHSG/index ticker in the price file). At least 126 date-aligned daily returns are needed for beta and residual volatility.
  • Valuation and financial ratios: fundamentals.csv with ticker,report_as_of,forward_pe,dividend_yield_ttm,roe,debt_to_equity. Add sector or peer_group (financial or non_financial) for Quality comparisons. Supply the ratios from reliable sources; the tool does not extract full financial statements or PDFs. Only positive forward P/E is inverted.
  • Alternative: a factor-input CSV with one row per ticker and raw columns earnings_yield,dividend_yield,momentum_12_1,price_vs_ma200,roe,debt_to_equity,beta_ihsg,residual_volatility, plus sector or peer_group. The template includes the fields needed to identify this format.

Use the CSV template ZIP for headers. Use decimal units for returns, yields, ROE and volatility (0.05 = 5%); P/E, beta and debt-to-equity are ratios. Use consistent corporate-action-adjusted closing prices. At least five eligible peers with nonzero cross-sectional variation are required for each component score; Quality uses separate financial and non-financial groups. Missing inputs remain unavailable, so some axes or points may not appear. Check financial-report availability against the price date yourself; the app does not reconstruct point-in-time history.

How to read a score. Each component is winsorized at the cross-sectional 5th/95th percentiles, converted to a population z-score, then clipped to −3…+3. Individual indicator axes use natural direction; Quality reverses DER and Low Volatility reverses beta and residual volatility. A complete factor score is the mean of both component z-scores; an incomplete factor is marked PARTIAL, and missing inputs are never replaced by zero.
