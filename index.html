Inputs and scoring logic

X Value

Earnings yield = 1 / verified positive forward P/E
Dividend yield = trailing 12-month yield

Each input is scored across eligible issuers; Value is the mean of the two component z-scores.

Y Momentum

12–1 return = close[t−21] / close[t−252] − 1
MA200 distance = close[t] / SMA200[t] − 1

Uses ordered daily closing prices. At least 253 closes are needed for these inputs.

Inputs come from company-report ratios. Quality is standardized separately for financial and non-financial issuers.

Color Low Volatility

IHSG beta, direction reversed
Daily OLS residual volatility, direction reversed

Estimated from issuer and IHSG daily returns aligned by date; at least 126 aligned returns are required.

How to read a score. Each component is winsorized at the cross-sectional 5th/95th percentiles, converted to a population z-score, then clipped to −3…+3. Lower DER, beta, and residual volatility receive higher scores. A complete factor score is the mean of both component z-scores; an incomplete factor is marked PARTIAL, and missing inputs are never replaced by zero.
